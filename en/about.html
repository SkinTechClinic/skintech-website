— About SkinTech

A premium clinic
for the considered client.

We are opening SkinTech because Amsterdam needs a different kind of aesthetic address — one where medical precision and boutique care are not in competition, but in conversation.

Our philosophy

Skin is specific. A scan, a protocol and a specialist who knows your name — that is the baseline. Everything else is decoration.

We treat one person at a time, in rooms that feel closer to an atelier than a waiting room. We say no to treatments you do not need. We measure, plan, and stay involved.

— Our approach

More than a treatment.

SkinTech was founded as a response — to chain clinics that process clients, to spas that lack clinical depth, and to a market that too often forces a choice between the two.

We wanted a space that treats aesthetic care with the same seriousness as any other branch of medicine, without losing the warmth that makes people want to return. A place where the technology is clinical-grade and the experience is genuinely beautiful.

Every detail — the lighting, the music, the scanner, the scheduling — is chosen to make skin treatment feel like a considered part of a considered life.
