Skin improvement in Amsterdam: where do you start?

From skin scan to care plan: what a skin clinic can do for you, and when it is better to see your GP.

More even, calmer or smoother skin: skin improvement is a broad term. What can a skin clinic do for you, what do you do yourself, and when is it better to see your GP? An overview, with what SkinTech is going to offer in Amsterdam.

Start with what your skin needs

No two skins are the same. That is why a course with us will start with a free skin scan and a conversation. The scan shows your skin in different kinds of light. The conversation is about what you want to achieve and what you already do. You go home with a written care plan.

How the scan works, and what it does and does not show, is explained in our article on the Observ 520x.

What a skin clinic can do

SkinTech focuses on three things:

  • Laser hair removal with the GentleMax Pro Plus, for long-term hair reduction. Read what you need to know before your first session.
  • Deep Cleansing Facial. A thorough cleanse by hand, for congested, oily or dull skin.
  • Glacē Glow Facial. Hydrodermabrasion with the Glacē by Candela, for dull or dehydrated skin.

Which facial suits you is explained in Deep Cleansing or Glacē Glow. These are cosmetic care treatments. We do not treat skin diseases.

Every skin, every skin type

People with every skin type live in Amsterdam, from very fair to very dark. For us that is a reason to work with a laser that, according to its American clearance, is intended for all skin types, and to treat a test patch first in a laser course.

You can read more in Skin type I to VI and in Laser hair removal for dark skin.

What you do yourself

What you do at home counts every day. Start with the sun. The Dutch Society for Dermatology and Venereology (NVDV) names shade and clothing as the first protection. For leisure and holidays the society advises a sunscreen with factor 30, applied thickly and again every few hours.

For the rest of your routine there is no advice that is right for everyone. That is why you get a personal care plan at the skin scan.

When to see your GP

Do you have a skin condition such as acne, eczema or rosacea, or a spot you are worried about? Then see your GP, who can refer you to a dermatologist if needed. A skin clinic is there for care and hair removal, not for a diagnosis or medical treatment.

When can you come?

SkinTech Laser Clinic opens in Amsterdam in the second quarter of 2027. Until then you can join the waiting list. The treatments and prices are on the Treatments page.
