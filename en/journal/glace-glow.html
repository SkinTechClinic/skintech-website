Glacē Glow Facial: how the treatment works

Hydrodermabrasion, a cupping massage and LED light: what the Candela Glacē does and what you can expect.

The Glacē Glow Facial will be one of the two facial treatments on our menu. It is built around the Glacē by Candela, a device for hydrodermabrasion. Below you can read what the device does, what the manufacturer says about it and what is not yet known.

What is the Glacē?

The Glacē is a system for facial treatments from Candela, the company that also makes our hair removal laser. Candela launched it in March 2025. The name is pronounced "glassy" and refers to glass skin, a term from Korean skincare for smooth, radiant skin.

According to Candela the treatment combines three things: hydrodermabrasion, a cupping massage with two modes and LED light.

The steps, according to the manufacturer

  • Cleansing and exfoliating. A tip with a diamond coating, meant for single use, moves over the skin and removes dead skin cells. At the same time a first serum rinses the skin clean.
  • Nourishing. A second serum follows, which nourishes the skin. Candela speaks of naturally derived serums, chosen for what your skin needs.
  • Cupping massage. Two cups massage both sides of the face at once. According to Candela one mode supports lymphatic drainage and the other gives the face a more sculpted look. The manufacturer itself adds that this effect is temporary.
  • Finishing. The treatment ends with a calming phase with warmth, cooling and LED light.

A treatment takes 30 to 60 minutes according to Candela, depending on the protocol. We will allow 45 to 60 minutes for it. We finish cool: alongside the Glacē we use the Cryo 7, a device by Zimmer that cools the skin with cold air.

What you can expect

Candela describes the treatment as suitable for many skin types and writes that there is little to no downtime. According to the manufacturer you see the effect, cleaner and softer skin, straight away. To keep it, Candela recommends regular treatments, for example every two to four weeks.

The manufacturer adds two things that matter to us. The treatment is not intended to treat skin diseases or medical conditions. And results vary from person to person. So this is care: cleansing, exfoliating and hydrating.

What is not yet known

The Glacē is new. Candela gives satisfaction figures, but they come from its own unpublished data, without numbers of participants or a study design. That is why we do not repeat them. We have not come across independent published research on the Glacē itself.

Something has been published on hydrodermabrasion as a technique, but the research is small in scale. A well-known example is a 2008 study with twenty participants and a different device.

Who it is for, and who it is not for

A Glacē Glow Facial suits anyone who wants to refresh dull or dehydrated skin, for example ahead of a special day or at the change of season.

Do you have a skin condition such as acne, eczema or rosacea, or do you use medication for your skin? Then discuss it with your GP or dermatologist first. If you are unsure whether to choose this treatment or our other facial, read Deep Cleansing or Glacē Glow.
