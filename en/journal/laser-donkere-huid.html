Laser hair removal for dark skin: what to look out for

Darker skin calls for a different laser. How the Nd:YAG wavelength makes the difference.

For a long time laser hair removal was seen as something for light skin with dark hair. That has changed. Dark skin does need the right laser, the right settings and someone with experience. This is what to know before you start.

Why dark skin needs extra care

A hair removal laser acts on pigment. The light has to reach the pigment of the hair, but dark skin also has a lot of pigment in its top layer. That pigment absorbs part of the energy. This makes side effects more likely: the Mayo Clinic names people with brown or black skin as a group at higher risk, and writes that dark skin needs the right laser and the right settings.

The Dutch Society for Dermatology and Venereology (NVDV) describes which side effects matter most here: light spots and dark spots, which can appear after days, weeks or months.

Which laser for dark skin

Lasers with a longer wavelength, such as the Nd:YAG laser (1064 nm), are considered the best choice for darker skin types. That is how researchers put it in a 2008 study of 59 women with skin types IV and V. After six treatments with an Nd:YAG laser they called the treatment safe and effective.

Other studies point the same way. A 2011 review of the records of 150 people with skin types IV to VI reached the same conclusion, with a satisfactory result for most participants. A 2001 study of twenty women with skin types IV to VI found damage to the hair follicles without damage to the top layer of the skin. These are small studies, mostly without a control group. They do not amount to a guarantee.

Questions to ask, at any clinic

  • Which laser do you use for my skin? Ask whether there is a long-wavelength laser, such as an Nd:YAG.
  • Do you have experience with my skin colour? The Mayo Clinic advises choosing someone who has experience with different skin colours.
  • Do you do a trial treatment first? That shows how your skin responds before a large area is treated.
  • What result can I expect? Laser hair removal reduces hair growth for a long time. Nobody can promise that every hair stays away for good.

The sun matters even more

Do not come to a treatment with tanned skin, and protect the treated skin well from the sun afterwards. The NVDV advises doing so for up to twelve weeks after the treatment, to prevent dark spots.
